# Moon

`moon.webp` is a decorative, AI-generated moon made with the built-in imagegen tool. It has a transparent background and is delivered at 768 × 768 pixels. The page dims it with CSS; it does not represent the current lunar phase or tunnel state.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural
> Asset type: decorative moon cutout for a minimal dark status website.
> Primary request: one remarkably realistic photographic Earth's moon, isolated on a genuinely transparent background.
> Subject: familiar near side of the Moon, almost full waxing gibbous, approximately 85 percent illuminated, with authentic lunar maria, subtle Tycho crater rays and fine pitted regolith detail. The curved terminator falls along the lower right edge. The unlit sliver is almost black.
> Composition/framing: a single complete circular lunar disc centered in a square image, occupying about 88 percent of its width, fully inside the image with a small even transparent margin. No other objects.
> Lighting/mood: natural sunlight from the upper left, restrained contrast, finely resolved craters along the terminator. Quiet, distant, photographic; realistic astronomical telephoto capture, not a fantasy planet or glossy 3D sphere.
> Color palette: neutral monochrome silver and charcoal, no blue or warm cast.
> Constraints: transparent background outside the lunar silhouette, crisp natural limb, no glow, no atmosphere, no stars, no clouds, no vignette, no added shadow outside the moon, no text, no border, no watermark. Preserve real physical texture and natural uneven surface shading. The asset will be displayed small and dimmed in CSS, so render the moon itself at natural photographic exposure.

The generated PNG was converted to WebP for delivery. No runtime image service or external request is needed. The Athletics fonts used by the page are separate and are not included in this directory.
